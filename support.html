Support

Stuck, found a bug, or want something TaskDriver doesn't do yet? Write to us. A person reads every message.

Last updated 6 November 2026

Contact us

Email im@nir.earth. We usually reply within a few days. Please tell us which app you're using (web, iPhone or Android), its version, and what you expected to happen.

Signing in

The web app, the iPhone app and the Android app all use the same account. Sign in with Google, and your tasks and spaces will be there on every device.

If you'd rather not use Google sign-in on a device, create an API token at app.taskdriver.io → Settings → Account → API tokens. In the app, choose Use API token and paste it in. You can revoke a token from the same screen at any time.

Sync and offline use

You can complete, edit and delete tasks with no connection. The changes are saved on your device and sync automatically once you're back online. Pull down on any list to refresh it.

If a task you created on another device doesn't appear, pull to refresh. If it's still missing, contact us.

Shared spaces

The owner of a space can open its menu, choose Share space, and invite people by email as viewers or editors, or turn on a share link. If someone shared a space with you, tap the "Shared with you" banner in that space to see who else is in it or to leave it.

Connected services

Connect Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, GitHub and Slack under Settings → Integrations. You can connect more than one account per service. Disconnecting a service revokes its access and deletes the data we cached from it. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what each service shares with us.

Deleting your account

To delete your account, email im@nir.earth from the address you sign in with. We'll delete your account and everything attached to it: tasks, spaces, comments and connected accounts. The Privacy Policy describes what happens to backups.
